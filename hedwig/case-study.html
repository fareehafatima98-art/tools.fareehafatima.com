From inboxes hitting spam to qualified sales conversations.

The situation

The company had a strong product and a clear market.

The problem wasn't the product.

Their outbound wasn't creating conversations.

Their email infrastructure was struggling with deliverability, with roughly half of outbound messages landing in spam.

That meant even good messaging couldn't do its job.

The team needed more than another sequence.

They needed the entire outbound motion diagnosed.

The problem

We identified several points where the outbound system was leaking:

Deliverability

Too much of the campaign never reached the prospect's primary inbox.

Messaging

The outreach wasn't consistently communicating a compelling reason for the prospect to engage.

Targeting

The right company wasn't always the same thing as the right prospect.

Value exchange

The prospect was being asked for time before receiving enough evidence that the conversation would be useful.

Follow-up

The sequence wasn't doing enough to build the case for responding.

What we changed

01

Diagnosed the outbound system

Instead of rewriting emails immediately, we looked at the entire journey from account selection to inbox placement to response.

02

Rebuilt the infrastructure

We addressed the underlying email setup and deliverability issues before scaling outreach.

03

Refined the targeting

We focused the campaign around prospects with a stronger reason to care about the problem.

04

Reworked the messaging

Messages were built around the prospect's situation rather than simply describing the company's product.

05

Added value before the meeting

Instead of relying entirely on a cold meeting request, we introduced useful assets that demonstrated the company's expertise before the prospect ever entered a sales conversation.

The result

Within the first phase of the campaign:

2 qualified sales conversations

And the number continued to increase as the system was iterated.

The bigger result wasn't simply two meetings.

It was proving that the company could move from:

"We're sending outbound but nobody is responding."

to:

"We're generating conversations with people who may actually buy."

What we learned

Outbound rarely fails because someone wrote the wrong sentence.

It fails because the system isn't working.

The right ICP with poor deliverability doesn't work.

Great deliverability with weak targeting doesn't work.

Perfect personalization without a compelling reason to engage doesn't work.

A strong offer without proof doesn't work.

The pieces have to work together.
